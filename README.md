# RobinCart — Fashion Dropshipping Storefront Starter

A responsive front-end storefront starter customized for an India-focused fashion and accessories brand.

## Run locally
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open `index.html` in a browser, or use VS Code with the Live Server extension.

## Customize before launch
- The storefront includes 150 sample catalogue entries: 50 Clothing, 50 Accessories, and 50 Bags & extras. Replace sample names, prices, and representative photos in `script.js` with supplier-approved product data before launch.
- Verify all photos, product descriptions, sizes, colours, stock, and image-use rights before publishing.
- Replace `hello@robincart.in` if that email address is not active.
- The promotion and customer reviews are sample content. Do not advertise an offer you cannot honour or publish invented testimonials.
- Add real shipping, returns, privacy, terms, contact, and grievance details appropriate to your business.

## Important: this is not a live commerce backend
The cart works in the browser, but orders are not saved, inventory is not managed, and checkout/payment is not connected. Before taking real orders, move the design to Shopify or connect a production WooCommerce setup, payment gateway, supplier order flow, and shipping/fulfilment process.

## Suggested India launch approach
- Start with a narrow edit (for example, everyday streetwear and accessories), not an uncurated catalogue.
- Compare Indian suppliers such as Qikink or Printrove if you want custom printed apparel, and ready-catalogue suppliers only after confirming current fashion range, branding, COD, returns, and fulfilment terms directly.
- Order samples across sizes and colours, check stitching/material/fit, and place a test delivery order to more than one pincode.
- Keep written supplier answers for dispatch time, stock sync, blind shipping/branding, customer invoices, COD settlement, exchange/return handling, RTO fees, damaged products, and tracking integration.
- Start with prepaid payments plus optional COD only after modelling return-to-origin (RTO) exposure and confirming supplier compatibility.
- Test the complete order path before running paid ads.

## Budget planning (indicative, not quotes)
For a ₹30,000–₹60,000 first launch, consider reserving roughly ₹3,000–₹6,000 for domain/theme essentials, ₹2,000–₹6,000 for samples and shipping, ₹2,000–₹5,000 for store setup/apps, ₹8,000–₹15,000 for initial marketing tests, and the balance for refunds, COD/RTO, packaging, and unexpected expenses. Shopify subscription, payment fees, courier charges, supplier costs, GST/tax advice, and ads vary; verify all current charges before paying.
